Our Approach

A gentler first step towards getting support

Starting a conversation about mental wellbeing can feel difficult. TrueSelf provides several ways to begin, depending on what feels most comfortable for you.

🕊️

Express Yourself

Share what is on your mind without needing to explain everything perfectly.

🔒

Privacy Focused

Use private chat and anonymous community features within the prototype.

🤝

Community Support

Connect with people who may understand similar feelings and experiences.

🌱

Support at Your Pace

Begin with a small check-in and move towards more suitable support when you feel ready.

♡

Your safety matters

TrueSelf is a student prototype and does not replace professional counselling, medical treatment or emergency services. In an immediate emergency, contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest emergency department.
